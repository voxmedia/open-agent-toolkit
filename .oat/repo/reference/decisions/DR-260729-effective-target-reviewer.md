# Effective-target reviewer notices

## Context

Bundled recommendation metadata can differ from preserved or layered effective configuration, and OAT cannot establish model access or organization-specific retention-policy eligibility.

## Decision

Describe the bundled terminal reviewer in policy choices, derive adoption and runtime notices from the effective configured or resolved target, and keep Fable access and retention language advisory.

## Consequences

Disclosures follow the target that will actually run without overstating eligibility; organizations remain responsible for confirming access and applicable retention policy.
