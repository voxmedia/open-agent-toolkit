- **Deny-list negation lookbehind requires a bare-word negation, so an emphasized
  strengthening trips the tripwire**
  (`packages/cli/src/commands/init/tools/shared/post-implement-sequence-contracts.test.ts:1005-1007`)
  - Issue: the lookbehind `(?<!never |not |do not |cannot )` matches only a
    literal lowercase-insensitive word plus one space immediately before the
    softener. Markdown emphasis breaks the adjacency. Reviewer probe STRONGBOLD
    inserted `**Never** treat the stop branch as advisory.` — a _strengthening_
    of the stop-and-report duty — into the sweep subsection, and the suite went
    **red, 1 failed / 34 passed, exit 1**, on the `not.toMatch` deny-list. The
    unbolded control (probe STRONG, `Never treat the stop branch as advisory.`)
    correctly stays green at 35/35, so the guard works for the form the inline
    comment names but not for the emphasized form. Since the surrounding prose
    already uses bold on its two decision bullets (`**Widen mechanically and
proceed**`, `**Stop and report**`), bolding a negation is a plausible future
    edit, and the failure would read as a contract violation when the author had
    in fact tightened the contract. The inline comment at `:993-995`
    ("so that _strengthening_ the duty … does not trip it") is therefore slightly
    broader than what the regex delivers.
  - Impact is fail-closed and low: it produces a spurious CI failure, never a
    silent acceptance, and the `not.toMatch` output points straight at the regex.
  - Fix (optional; may also route to the wave-level contract-test item): widen the
    lookbehind to tolerate adjacent markup, e.g.
    `(?<!(?:never|not|do not|cannot)[\s*_`]{1,4})` (JavaScript supports
    variable-length lookbehind), and add the bolded strengthening as a green
    control beside the existing probes. Alternatively soften the inline comment to
    say the lookbehind covers bare-word negations only.
