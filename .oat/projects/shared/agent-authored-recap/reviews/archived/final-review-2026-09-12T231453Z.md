    status the page states to trace by subject and value. The current bypass

    emphasized card label, so the residual guard is not fully fail-closed.

    residual label text under the resolved closed subject. Avoid counting the
    identifier's own digits as a new fact unless source indexing uses the same
    rule. Add real-verifier controls for composite facts in at least
    `strong` and `dt`, retain the three bare unlabeled failures, assert no
