### Important

- **A recorded required-pass failure does not invalidate the same pass's complete artifact** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:989`)
  - Issue: `validateApprovedLanes` stops checking a lane as soon as a complete artifact exists, even when the manifest also contains a material `PASS_FAILED` or `PASS_OMITTED` gap for that pass. `collectCompletePasses` then counts the artifact without reconciling failure evidence, and `deriveAchievedProfile` preserves the full profile (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:1085`, `.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:1136`). A direct production-API probe kept the valid thorough fixture's complete redundant-gather dossier, set the run to `partial`, and added a material `PASS_FAILED` gap naming `redundant-gather`; `validatePacket` returned `valid: true`, `publishable: true`, and `achievedProfile: "thorough"` with no errors. This contradicts the accepted-lane contract that a failed lane stays failed and material (`.agents/skills/recon/SKILL.md:230`, `.oat/projects/shared/recon-rework/design.md:193`) and the p-rev6 requirement that failure cannot preserve the thorough profile (`.oat/projects/shared/recon-rework/plan.md:1274`). A stale or leftover complete artifact can therefore mask the root-recorded failure at the publication boundary.
  - Fix: Reconcile typed artifacts with material pass-outcome gaps before deriving achievement. Reject a packet that contains both a complete artifact and `PASS_FAILED`/`PASS_OMITTED` evidence for the same approved pass, or make the failure outcome dominate so that the pass is excluded and the achieved profile degrades. Add production-boundary regressions for both primary `gather` and `redundant-gather`, plus a valid partial control containing failure evidence without a complete artifact.
