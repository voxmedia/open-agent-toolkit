### Important

- **Thorough routing performs redundant gathering too late to affect synthesis or review** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/lib/contracts.mjs:124`)
  - Issue: The enforced singleton order is `map`, `gather`, `compile`, the standard review waves, and only then `redundant-gather` (`contracts.mjs:124-135`). That contradicts the controller pipeline, which requires map and gathering workers to produce dossiers before `compile` creates the candidate ledger and before review briefs are created (`.agents/skills/recon/SKILL.md:237-250`). The fixture makes the gap observable: it writes the ledger with only the first dossier in `inputArtifacts` (`packet-fixture.mjs:338-344`), creates the redundant-gather dossier later without connecting it to that ledger (`packet-fixture.mjs:610-615`, `658-684`), and allows reconciliation to consume only review artifacts (`packet-fixture.mjs:557-584`). The validator nevertheless derives `redundant-gather` solely from two complete gather-lane artifacts (`validate-packet.mjs:1051-1057`). As a result, a packet can achieve `thorough` even though the redundant source extraction was never compiled, exposed to independent review, or incorporated by reconciliation. A direct topology probe confirmed the current late order is accepted while moving `redundant-gather` before `compile` is rejected with `OUT_OF_ORDER_PROFILE_TOPOLOGY`.
  - Fix: Move the thorough profile's `redundant-gather` wave before `compile`, then update the routing fixtures and integration tests so the candidate ledger demonstrably incorporates both independent gather outputs before briefs are created. Add a negative control that omits the redundant dossier from the compiled ledger's inputs/provenance and requires rejection (or, if a different consumer is intended, define and validate that consumer explicitly).
  - Requirement: The thorough profile promises “redundant independent gathering and redundant verification for load-bearing claims” (`.agents/skills/recon/references/profiles.md:45-58`), and the controller contract requires gathering before compilation (`.agents/skills/recon/SKILL.md:237-250`).
