### Important

- **Pass-level contradiction check makes mixed-outcome multi-lane waves unpublishable** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:1146`)
  - Issue: `reconcilePassOutcomes` deletes a pass and records
    `CONTRADICTORY_PASS_OUTCOME` whenever any material `PASS_FAILED`/`PASS_OMITTED`
    gap satisfies `gapNamesMode(gap, mode)` (line 1149). For non-conditional
    lanes, `validateApprovedLanes` requires a lane without a complete artifact
    to be covered by a gap satisfying the identical predicate
    `gapNamesMode(gap, wave.mode)` (line 999) or it fails with
    `MISSING_LANE_OUTCOME`. The two rules are therefore mutually exclusive for
    any wave with at least one complete lane and at least one failed/cancelled
    lane: the gap the lane contract demands is the gap the pass contract
    rejects. Quick's documented 1–4 adaptive `gather` lanes
    (`.agents/skills/recon/references/profiles.md:15`), and every adaptive
    multi-lane mode in standard/thorough, lose the honest-partial path after an
    accepted worker failure that `profiles.md:78`, `SKILL.md:61-72` (the
    `worker` failure category), `packet-contract.md:377-379`,
    `apps/oat-docs/docs/workflows/skills/recon.md:254-260`, and discovery
    decision 8 ("Partial outcomes remain visible") all describe as valid. The
    change also flipped an existing positive control rather than adding a new
    negative one: `.agents/skills/recon/tests/integrity-contracts.test.mjs:407-423`
    previously asserted that quick with `lane-gather-1` complete and
    `lane-gather-2` cancelled (gap "gather lane lane-gather-2 was cancelled
    before writing.") is `valid: true` at `achievedProfile: 'quick'`; it now
    asserts `valid: false`, `achievedProfile: null`, and
    `CONTRADICTORY_PASS_OUTCOME`. The prior review's probe
    (`reviews/archived/final-review-2026-09-11T183428Z.md`, Important) was a
    single-lane `redundant-gather` wave, where complete-plus-failed genuinely is
    a contradiction; the fix generalized that to every lane of every wave.
  - Fix: Reconcile at lane granularity, not pass granularity. Give
    non-conditional outcome gaps the same exact `waveId`/`laneId` identity the
    conditional path already uses (`gapNamesConditionalLane`, line 710), and
    in `reconcilePassOutcomes` treat a pass as contradicted only when a
    complete artifact and a material gap name the same lane (or when a
    mode-only gap has no lane identity and the wave has exactly one lane).
    Keep the pass achieved when at least one lane is complete and the failed
    lane is covered by a lane-identified gap; the material gap already forces
    `partial`. Restore the `integrity-contracts.test.mjs` multi-lane scenario as
    the positive control, and keep the single-lane `gather`/`redundant-gather`
    contradiction tests as the negative controls. If the product decision is
    instead that any lane failure fails the whole pass, then that is a contract
    change: document it in `packet-contract.md` and `profiles.md`, and
    re-examine whether "1–4 gather lanes" with honest partial publication is
    still the advertised behavior.
  - Requirement: discovery decision 8; `design.md` section 4 failure
    preservation ("stays failed and material", not "invalidates the run");
    plan `prev7-t01` Step 2 (reject the contradictory combination for the
    same approved pass).

### Medium

- **Packet contract does not state the new complete-artifact-versus-gap rule** (`.agents/skills/recon/references/packet-contract.md:209-217`)
  - Issue: The contract documents the lane outcome rule (`MISSING_LANE_OUTCOME`,
    `UNAPPROVED_LANE`, `LANE_WRITE_PATH_VIOLATION`) and the new
    `MISSING_THOROUGH_GATHER_LEDGER_INPUT` (line 203), but nothing describes
    that a complete artifact coexisting with a material `PASS_FAILED`/
    `PASS_OMITTED` gap naming the same pass is now rejected with
    `CONTRADICTORY_PASS_OUTCOME`. Controllers author these gaps from
    `SKILL.md`, so an undocumented rejection rule at the publication boundary
    is a contract gap that will surface as unexplained packet failures. The
    p-rev4 `CONDITION_EVIDENCE_RUN_MISMATCH` rule is described in prose (lines
    141-143) but its code is likewise unnamed.
  - Fix: After resolving the Important finding, add one sentence to the lane
    outcome paragraph stating exactly when a complete artifact and a material
    outcome gap are contradictory and which code results, and name
    `CONDITION_EVIDENCE_RUN_MISMATCH` alongside the same-run requirement. Pin
    the sentence in `skill-contract.test.mjs` as the project has done for other
    contract clauses.

### Minor

- **Ledger-input check falls back to the final ledger when reconciliation is missing** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:2429`)
  - Issue: `validateThoroughGatherLedgerInputs` receives
    `reconciliationContext.priorLedger ?? ledger`. When a thorough packet lacks
    its terminal reconciliation (already reported as `SHADOW_RECONCILIATION`),
    the fallback inspects the final ledger, whose `inputArtifacts` reference the
    prior ledger rather than dossiers, so `MISSING_THOROUGH_GATHER_LEDGER_INPUT`
    also fires as a derivative diagnostic. The project's p-rev4 and p05-t03 work
    deliberately gives each defect one diagnostic owner.
  - Suggestion: Skip the ledger-input check when `reconciliationRequired` is
    true and `priorLedger` is null, or scope the fallback to quick/standard
    where the final ledger is the compile output.
