### Important

- **Primary gather lanes can impersonate the failed redundant-gather pass** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:1098`)
  - Issue: `collectCompletePasses()` derives `redundant-gather` whenever any two complete raw dossiers with wire mode `gather` have distinct lane IDs (`validate-packet.mjs:1098-1104`); it does not require either dossier to belong to the approved `redundant-gather` wave. The new ledger-input validator then skips an approved gather lane when that lane has no complete dossier (`validate-packet.mjs:1032-1044`). A direct production-boundary reproduction used two complete lanes in the primary `gather` wave, included both exact path/digest references in the candidate ledger, removed the redundant-wave dossier, and recorded a material `PASS_FAILED` gap for `redundant-gather`. With `run.status: partial`, the current validator returned `valid: true`, `publishable: true`, and derived `achievedProfile: thorough` with no errors. The failed redundant wave therefore remains visible as a gap but does not lower assurance, so verified claims can retain the thorough profile without the required independent redundant gathering.
  - Fix: Derive the `gather` and `redundant-gather` passes from the approved wave that owns each lane, not from the cardinality of all dossiers whose artifact-level mode is `gather`. A material failure gap may permit a partial packet to publish, but it must not satisfy the failed pass or allow the manifest to claim `thorough`. Add a regression with two complete primary gather lanes plus a failed/omitted redundant-gather lane, exact candidate-ledger inputs, and otherwise complete thorough evidence; require a categorical profile/pass mismatch instead of a publishable thorough result. Cover the reciprocal case as well so redundant-wave lanes cannot impersonate the primary gather pass.
  - Requirement: Thorough requires standard topology plus redundant independent gathering (`.agents/skills/recon/references/profiles.md:50`), and the controller requires completed primary and redundant gather waves before compilation (`.agents/skills/recon/SKILL.md:239`).
