### Minor

- **The blockquote fix does not reach a blockquoted _ordered_ list marker**
  (`.agents/skills/codex-skill/tests/codex-skill-contract.test.mjs:157`)
  - Issue: `clauseOpener` now strips `>` prefixes, but the parallel
    `LIST_MARKER_ONLY = /^(?:[-*+]|\d+[.)])$/` filter in `splitClauses` was not
    updated with it. A blockquoted ordered marker ends in `.`, so `> 1.` splits
    off as its own clause, fails `LIST_MARKER_ONLY` (because of the `>`),
    survives the filter as a non-anaphoric clause, and breaks adjacency before
    the real anaphor is reached. Probe N5,
    `${belowFloor} > 1. In that case, confirm before launching.`, is ACCEPTED.
    The unquoted counterpart is pinned and correctly rejected
    ("anaphor behind an ordered list marker"), and `> - In that case, …` (N4's
    sibling M6) is rejected because a `-` marker does not end in `.` and so never
    splits. A second, lower-realism sibling: `- > In that case, …` (N4) is
    ACCEPTED because the blockquote strip runs before the list-marker strip and
    therefore never sees a `>` that sits _behind_ a list marker.
  - Fix (optional, one line): allow a quoted prefix in the marker filter —
    `const LIST_MARKER_ONLY = /^(?:>\s*)*(?:[-*+]|\d+[.)])$/;` — and pin
    `${belowFloor} > 1. In that case, confirm before launching.` as a rejected
    case. Mitigating context, and why this is Minor rather than Medium: it
    requires a blockquote **and** an ordered marker **and** an anaphor, and no
    `SKILL.md` under `.agents/skills` contains a blockquote at all (0 blockquote
    lines in `codex-skill/SKILL.md`, no `> [!` callout anywhere in the family).
    Reasonable to defer to `BL-260827-span-based-prose-guards` alongside m2.
