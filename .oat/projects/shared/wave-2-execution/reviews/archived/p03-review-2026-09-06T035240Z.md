### Important

- **Helper comment claims contradiction-resistance the patterns do not provide**
  (`.agents/skills/explainer-kit/tests/contracts.test.mjs:806-811`)

  Issue: the comment asserts "prose that names a token while asserting the
  opposite rule does not pass," and the phase report repeats this as a fixed
  Codex finding. Sentence scoping only constrains **token order within a
  sentence**; it does not detect negation. Four token-preserving inversions I
  wrote are all **accepted** by `assertPublicationBoundary`:

  ```text
  B1  "...never requires the complete post-run `publish-receipt/v2` and"
  B2  "does not validate complete manifest and catalog evidence before it accepts a run."
  B3  "A `publish-receipt/v1` is the only receipt rewritten in place after replay."
  B4  "A `publish-receipt/v1` may be mutated; only a full replay is then required."
  ```

  B1 and B2 place the negation **before** the anchor token, so ordering is
  unaffected. B3 and B4 assert v1 is **mutable** — the exact inverse of the
  guarded rule — yet satisfy the v1 pattern because its `only` lookahead is
  sentence-wide and need not modify `replay`. The implementer's own two
  contradiction fixtures (`contracts.test.mjs:889-903`) happen to place the
  inverting words **after** the anchor token, which is the one arrangement
  ordering catches. The guard is stronger than the pre-change version, but not
  in the way the comment states, and a reader will over-trust it.

  Fix: either (a) narrow the comment to the guarantee actually held — the tokens
  must appear in one sentence in this order, which catches reordering and
  dropped tokens but not negation — or (b) strengthen the patterns: bind `only`
  adjacent to `replay` rather than sentence-wide, and add a `doesNotMatch` for
  mutation verbs (`rewritten`, `mutated`, `overwritten`) occurring in the same
  sentence as `publish-receipt/v1`. Add the four fixtures above so the claim is
  enforced.

  Requirement: Review focus "Inspect the negative case for vacuity".
