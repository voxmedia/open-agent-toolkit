### Medium

- **M1: Rebuild each review brief once per assurance pass, rather than once per claim** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:2143`).

  `validateAssurance` loops over verified claims and their review IDs, and
  `checkReviewBrief` inside that loop regenerates, validates, and hashes the
  entire brief. A standard packet with N verified claims therefore rebuilds
  each of its three N-claim briefs N times, even though
  `validateReviewBindings` already checked those briefs. The new whole-brief
  comparison amplifies the previous per-claim binding cost.

  Reproduction with valid helper-produced packets: at 100 / 400 / 800 claims,
  the current validator took 241 / 1,188 / 4,530 ms, versus 30 / 95 / 244 ms
  with the pre-p03-t09 validator and its binding/omission helpers. Both
  implementations accepted every packet with no errors. Doubling from 400 to
  800 claims nearly quadrupled current validation time; the 800-claim pass
  was about 18.6 times slower. Rendering invokes validation again, so this
  cost also affects publication. Timings are local observations, not portable
  performance thresholds.

  Fix: compute the rebuild result once per distinct brief within each
  assurance pass and reuse its validity and entry-ID set for the claim
  checks. Keep the rebuild-and-compare guard, exact brief references,
  mode/run binding, disposition checks, and all existing tamper controls.
  Scope any cache to the current immutable validation generation.

  Verification: rerun the scaling probe below and the recon suite. The
  400-to-800 step should stop paying for repeated whole-brief reconstruction,
  while every brief-tamper row continues to reject with
  `REVIEW_BRIEF_MISMATCH`.
