Why does approval come after render and QA?

A reviewer cannot judge an artifact that has not been rendered, so the gate moved downstream of render and Render QA. A rejection does not discard the run: the accepted artifact set is persisted in the approval record, so an edit resumes and re-renders without re-invoking the author.
