  - Issue: The no-match branch now creates a new gated preview, but
    `buildCreateProjection()` always includes title and priority and only applies

    local/state change between no-match and approval therefore does not invalidate
    the create handoff.
  - Fix: Build recreate fields from `composePurposePolicies()` plus the current
    effective description/priority policy. At apply time, recompute effective
    policy and the complete current preimage, reconstruct the preview components,
    and reject any drift before handing off the persisted action. Add purpose,

  - Requirement: FR3, FR7, FR8, FR14, NFR1, NFR2; p07-t06 and the authorized
    recovery.

- **Resolution observations can be stranded by a crash after the current action
  is retired** (`packages/cli/src/commands/pjm/remote/service.ts:4035`)
  - Issue: Uncertain/rejected outcomes, non-success duplicate outcomes, the
    found-existing handoff, and the no-match create-approval handoff unlink the
    durable current-action pointer before persisting the corresponding terminal or
    next operation state (`service.ts:4040-4064`, `:4077-4098`, `:4117-4137`,
    `:4203-4233`). `retireCurrentAction()` physically unlinks that pointer
    (`store.ts:311-350`), while continuation has no resolution fallback from the
    immutable action-evidence file (`service.ts:2928-2959`). A crash in these
    windows leaves the old operation awaiting an action that can no longer be
    continued. The uncertain freeze itself is a metadata/state split write, and
    detach's bridge updates metadata time without updating
    `state.metadataUpdatedAt` (`service.ts:1625-1665`), which doctor correctly
    diagnoses as `metadata-timestamp` (`doctor.ts:304-305`). The admitted absence
    of a resolution-specific crash matrix therefore conceals actual restart and
    coherence defects.
  - Fix: Journal the accepted observation and intended next/terminal state before
    retiring the pointer, make continuation reconstruct resolution handoffs from
    immutable action/observation evidence, and move freeze and detach through
    restart-safe materialization with coherent timestamps. Inject crashes at every
    resolution observation, freeze, metadata, state, action-pointer, and terminal
    boundary and prove replay/repair.
  - Requirement: FR13, FR14, NFR2, NFR3; p07-t05, p07-t06, p07-t09.

- **Relink and recreate replace rather than retain the former complete snapshot**
  (`packages/cli/src/commands/pjm/remote/service.ts:4381`)
  - Issue: Successful relink/recreate constructs a fresh snapshot and assigns it
    directly to the single `state.snapshot` slot (`service.ts:4381-4405`). The only
    remnant of the former snapshot is its digest in operation verification
    (`service.ts:4417-4423`); the actual allowlisted core snapshot is not retained
    anywhere before common materialization overwrites it. A digest cannot satisfy
    the explicit requirement to retain the last complete local core/former
    snapshot for lifecycle recovery.
  - Fix: Persist the complete bounded former snapshot as immutable resolution
    evidence or in a versioned snapshot history before replacing the current
    snapshot. Bind both former and replacement records to the resolution journal,
    make materialization idempotent, and test content-level recovery across every
    crash point.
  - Requirement: FR5, FR13, NFR3; p07-t05 and p07-t06.

    publication/closeout workflows through real stores and action/observation
