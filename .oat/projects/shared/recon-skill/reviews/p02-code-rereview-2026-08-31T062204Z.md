## Findings

### Critical

- **Review results can verify a claim that the immutable brief never contained** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:783`)
  - Issue: validation resolves an exact hashed brief and checks only its run ID and mode. It never requires the reviewed claim ID, statement, cited evidence, or locator projection to appear in that brief before accepting the result's disposition. The checked-in fixtures demonstrate the bypass: `packet-fixture.mjs:167` creates a verification brief with `claims: []`, `packet-fixture.mjs:180` creates an adversarial brief with `provisionalStatements: []`, and the results nevertheless disposition `claim-1` at `packet-fixture.mjs:209`. A fresh direct probe confirmed that this packet validates as `standard` with a `verified` claim.
  - Fix: for semantic results, require the exact claim ID, statement, evidence IDs, excerpts, locators, and source descriptors in the referenced verification brief; for adversarial and coverage results, require the claim ID and statement in the correct immutable projection. Reject dispositions for absent or content-drifted claims and give coverage its own correctly typed brief/projection if its permitted input differs from adversarial review.
  - Requirement: Independently reviewed categorical assurance and immutable selective-blind review inputs.

- **Achieved profiles still trust unbound inline stage completion** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:556`)
  - Issue: only semantic, adversarial, coverage, and reconciliation stages require any typed artifact; every other stage is complete solely because `stage.status === "complete"`. A fresh thorough fixture validated with empty `artifactIds` for `redundant-gather`, `redundant-verification`, and `contradiction-resolution`, and with zero dispatch receipts. Quick map/gather/compile/locator stages are likewise not bound to their outputs. This preserves the original false-assurance class for the core and all thorough-only passes even though review-stage artifacts are now checked.
  - Fix: define the required artifact kinds and cardinality for every stage mode, require every `artifactId` to resolve to a hashed artifact from the same run, bind the stage to accepted/completed dispatch receipts and expected lanes, and derive each profile only from those validated stage outcomes. Add negative tests for empty/missing/wrong-kind outputs and absent, failed, wrong-run, or unaccepted receipts for every required stage, especially all thorough-only stages.
  - Requirement: Validator-derived requested-versus-achieved rigor and evidence-backed pass completion.

- **Unavailable or invalid sources remain assurance-eligible** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:241`)
  - Issue: `reopenEvidence` checks minimum provenance and the evidence locator state, but never checks `source.available` or `source.validationState`. A fresh probe changed a valid file source to `available: false` and `validationState: "invalid"`; the quick packet still returned `valid: true` with a `supported` claim and no errors. This contradicts the source contract and the success criterion that stale or inaccessible sources cannot retain assurance.
  - Fix: close the source availability/validation-state vocabulary and gate reopening/assurance on an eligible source state. Require unavailable, stale, invalid, or otherwise non-pinned sources to produce explicit gaps and prevent their evidence from supporting `supported` or `verified` claims. Add positive and negative tests across all source discriminators.
  - Requirement: Source-bound evidence must degrade honestly when stale, inaccessible, or invalid.

- **Reconciliation does not validate transitions against the prior ledger** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:627`)
  - Issue: the validator confirms only that the prior artifact has the declared revision, the output revision increments by one, and the reconciliation transition array byte-matches the current ledger. It never compares each transition's `from` state with the corresponding prior claim or verifies claim/evidence continuity. A fresh probe changed the bound prior claim to `unsupported` while the current ledger declared `supported -> verified`; validation still returned `valid: true` with no errors.
  - Fix: index both ledgers by claim ID, require every transition's `from` to equal the prior status and `to` to equal the current status, reject missing/duplicate transitions, and enforce allowed additions/removals plus statement/evidence/qualification continuity. Require reconciliation to account for every current change and prohibit evidence invention.
  - Requirement: Only reconciliation may produce a new ledger through legal, auditable state transitions.
