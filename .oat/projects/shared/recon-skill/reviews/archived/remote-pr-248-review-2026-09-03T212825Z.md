## Findings

### I1: Uncertain reviews leave claims unsettled

- **Severity:** important
- **Source:** `github_pr`
- **Comment:** `3928744709`
- **Location:** `.agents/skills/recon/scripts/reconcile-ledger.mjs:258`
- **Source ref:** <https://github.com/voxmedia/open-agent-toolkit/pull/248#discussion_r3928744709>
- **Disposition:** convert to `p06-t01`

`reconcileLedger` ignores `uncertain` and incomplete review sets and leaves the
prior claim status unchanged. Compile output is revision-one `provisional`, so
those claims stay `provisional` on revision two. `legalReconciliationTransitions`
also omits `provisional:unresolved`, while ledger validation forbids leftover
provisional genesis. Standard and thorough reconciliation then throws
`INVALID_PROVISIONAL_GENESIS` instead of publishing an honest `unresolved`
partial.

### M1: Coverage gaps can mark claims verified

- **Severity:** medium
- **Source:** `github_pr`
- **Comment:** `3928744724`
- **Location:** `.agents/skills/recon/scripts/reconcile-ledger.mjs:264`
- **Source ref:** <https://github.com/voxmedia/open-agent-toolkit/pull/248#discussion_r3928744724>
- **Disposition:** convert to `p06-t02`

`coreComplete` treats a coverage disposition of `gap` as satisfying the required
`covered` review. Promotion then uses only a material coverage finding to choose
`contested` versus `verified`. A `gap` without a material finding therefore
becomes `verified`, which later packet assurance rejects because verified
claims require `covered`.

### M2: Adversary briefs skip statement checks

- **Severity:** medium
- **Source:** `github_pr`
- **Comment:** `3920127560`
- **Location:** `.agents/skills/recon/scripts/create-review-brief.mjs:283`
- **Source ref:** <https://github.com/voxmedia/open-agent-toolkit/pull/248#discussion_r3920127560>
- **Disposition:** convert to `p06-t03`

`validateReviewBrief` enforces exact `id`/`statement` projections for `verify`
and `coverage`, but an `adversary` brief only checks that `questions`,
`provisionalStatements`, and `scope` exist. Extra fields on those statements can
pass this gate and be written by `writeReviewBrief`, so an adversary worker can
see claim status, evidence, or qualifications and lose selective blindness
before later packet validation.

### M3: Unresolved claims cannot become verified

- **Severity:** medium
- **Source:** `github_pr`
- **Comment:** `3920127563`
- **Location:** `.agents/skills/recon/scripts/lib/contracts.mjs:81`
- **Source ref:** <https://github.com/voxmedia/open-agent-toolkit/pull/248#discussion_r3920127563>
- **Disposition:** convert to `p06-t04`

`legalTransitions` allows `provisional` to jump to `verified` and allows
`unresolved` to become `supported`, `contested`, or `unsupported`, but not
`verified`. After independent reviews affirm a previously unsettled claim, the
ledger validator still rejects `unresolved` to `verified`, so that claim cannot
reach the intended assurance state in one reconciliation.
