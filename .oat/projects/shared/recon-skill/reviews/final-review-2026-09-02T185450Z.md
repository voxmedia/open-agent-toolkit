  - Fix: bind withdrawal to the retained packet-root identity. Add real-directory replacement and symlink-retarget negative controls proving an identity failure cannot remove any file from the replacement root, plus the existing unchanged-root withdrawal control.

### Important

  - Fix: retain byte identities/digests for the canonical manifest, ledger, and validated referenced packet artifacts when constructing `ValidatedRun`, then verify they are unchanged immediately before and after promotion. On mismatch, withdraw `packet.md` using the safe unchanged-root cleanup from C1. This is a bounded integrity check and does not require immutable-generation staging or a publication state machine. Add a deterministic canonical-mutation-during-promotion negative control and retain valid fresh/replacement success controls.
  - Requirement: Discovery directory-first packet contract, Design artifact pipeline and publication outcomes, and the Revision 5 split-generation objective.
