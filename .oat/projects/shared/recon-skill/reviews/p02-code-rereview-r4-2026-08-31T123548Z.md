## Findings

### Critical

- **The exact approval and dispatch selection axes can be erased while the packet remains publishable** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/lib/contracts.mjs:183`)
  - Issue: `validateApprovalEnvelope` requires only `waves`. Dispatch receipts require `selection` and `approvalEnvelope` objects, but lines 1364-1413 only close their permitted keys and do not require provider, model, effort, route, role, service tier, authority, or execution caps. A fresh standard-packet mutation reduced the manifest envelope to `{waves: [...]}`, reduced every receipt selection to `{}`, updated the canonical fingerprints, and still validated, rendered, and achieved `standard`. The fingerprint at `validate-packet.mjs:1612` therefore authenticates an incomplete envelope rather than proving the exact approved execution contract required by the design.
  - Fix: Define one required canonical approval-envelope schema covering every design-required selection/execution axis and require it consistently in the manifest, approved/accepted/completed receipts, and accepted envelope. Require the receipt selection fields, prove equality across every stage receipt and the manifest, and add axis-deletion mutations for every required field.
  - Requirement: Discovery exact-model/effort approval; design execution-manifest and no-substitution contract; p02-t01/p02-t02.

- **A shadow reconciliation lets a removal review bind a different prior claim than the terminal reconciliation removes** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:805`)
  - Issue: `validateReviewBindings` selects the first artifact whose review kind is `reconciliation` and uses its input ledger to validate prior-only claim dispositions. The terminal reconciliation is independently selected from the completed reconciliation stage at lines 945-947. A fresh mutation added an extra, unreceipted reconciliation pointing to a forged prior ledger whose contested claim had the same ID but different content; the receipted semantic rejection reviewed that forged content, while the real terminal reconciliation removed the actual prior claim. The packet validated without errors. Thus the typed-removal check at lines 1028-1047 proves only a matching claim ID, not authorization against the actual terminal reconciliation's immutable prior ledger.
  - Fix: Resolve the terminal reconciliation exactly once from the required completed stage and pass that same object/prior-ledger identity into every review-binding and transition/removal check. Reject every additional reconciliation result not exactly bound to that stage and receipts. Bind removal authorization to the actual prior claim's statement, evidence, qualifications, and immutable brief.
  - Requirement: Packet contract next-revision continuity and typed removal authorization; p02-t02.

- **Auditable stale/unavailable/invalid evidence bypasses secret redaction and can publish the raw secret** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:248`)
  - Issue: `reopenEvidence` returns immediately when `allowIneligibleAudit` is true, before it validates the locator or reaches the `UNREDACTED_SECRET` check at lines 536-545. The caller grants that bypass when a gap names the source/affected downgraded claims. A fresh stale-source mutation supplied the exact material gap and legal claim downgrade, then persisted a secret-like `api_key` excerpt; validation and rendering both succeeded, and `render-packet.mjs:95-97` copied the secret-bearing `displayExcerpt` into `packet.md`. A source becoming ineligible must lower assurance, not disable the persistence safety boundary.
  - Fix: Separate assurance eligibility from persistence safety. For ineligible audited evidence, still validate/redact every persisted excerpt and diagnostic before accepting it; never render a raw secret. If exact reopening is impossible, require a safely redacted/non-exact audit representation whose digest and locator semantics cannot advance assurance. Add stale, invalid, and unavailable secret-bearing mutation tests for validator errors, diagnostics, and rendered output.
  - Requirement: Design evidence drift and secret-redaction contract; p02-t02/p02-t03.

### Important

- **A stale source with a non-material gap can publish a `complete` packet** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/validate-packet.mjs:1311`)
  - Issue: Source eligibility checks accept any matching gap, and the ineligible-audit lookup at lines 1667-1674 likewise does not require `material: true`. Complete status is rejected only when a gap is material at lines 1559-1568. A fresh standard-packet mutation marked the source stale, downgraded all affected claims to `contested`, supplied a complete but non-material source gap, retained `run.status: complete`, and validated/rendered successfully. This contradicts the packet contract's explicit one-material-gap rule and the design's requirement that drifted evidence publish partial or fail.
  - Fix: Require the exact stale/unavailable/invalid source gap to be material whenever it affects claims/evidence used by the canonical ledger, and require `partial` (or fail if no valid base remains). Cross-check categorical gap materiality against source eligibility rather than trusting caller classification.

- **A repository source may declare a symlink alias as its canonical root** (`.agents/skills/recon/scripts/lib/safe-path.mjs:14`)
  - Issue: `assertNoSymlinkComponents` checks only components below `resolvedRoot`; it does not `lstat` the root itself before `realpath` at line 43. `validate-packet.mjs:300-312` treats the declared source root as its own trust anchor. A fresh quick-packet mutation replaced the repository root with a symlink alias to the real fixture root, updated the locator content hash, and remained publishable. This defeats the declared canonical-root identity and leaves root retargeting outside the symlink guard even though ancestor/final components below the root are rejected.
  - Fix: Require repository/file/capture/output roots to be canonical absolute realpaths and reject a declared root that is itself a symlink. Bind the canonical real root into source provenance/digests and recheck it before reading and publication. Add root-symlink and root-retarget mutation tests for every managed path class.
