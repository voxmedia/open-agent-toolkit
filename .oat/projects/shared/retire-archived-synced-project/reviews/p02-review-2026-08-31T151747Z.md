  (`.agents/skills/oat-project-complete/scripts/parse-synced-archive-resume-fields.mjs:46`)
  - Issue: Every archive resume sets `EVIDENCE_COMMIT` to empty, including a
    recordless retry after Step 10.6 created the recap evidence commit but a
    later push or PR-closeout operation failed. Because resume skips Step 7.5,
    no later step recovers that receipt. Step 10.6 then says to create an
    evidence update whenever Step 10.5 ran without a restored
    `EVIDENCE_COMMIT` (`SKILL.md:1270`), even though the exported manifest may
    already be durable and the exact-path evidence commit already exists. At
    best, the instruction is ambiguous between replaying a now-empty commit and
    silently skipping it; at worst, retry stops on the duplicate commit. The
    final push also falls back to `LIFECYCLE_COMMIT` (`SKILL.md:1346`), so its
    upstream postcondition does not prove that the recap evidence commit was
    pushed. This violates the required recordless downstream retry and exact
    bookkeeping-push contract for the recap shape. The new recordless test uses
    no recap and no evidence interruption, so it cannot detect this gap.
  - Fix: On recordless recap resume, locate and validate the optional evidence
    commit as the exact two-path immediate child of `LIFECYCLE_COMMIT`, determine
    whether its push is still required, and export those receipts into the
    continuation. Make Step 10.5/10.6 routing explicit: a recovered evidence
    commit must skip attestation/commit replay and be the exact commit verified
    in the upstream; a missing or mismatched candidate must fail closed. Add
    recordless recap retry tests for interruption after evidence commit before
    push and after evidence push.
  - Requirement: p02-t02/p02-t03 idempotent interruption recovery and the fresh
    recap/no-recap downstream retry contract.

- **Tracked-PR update failure still clears the recovery pointer**
  (`.agents/skills/oat-project-complete/SKILL.md:1430`)
  - Issue: For an archived project whose persisted status identifies an open
    PR, Step 11.5 is applicable. Its failure handling still says that a missing
    `gh` or failed `gh pr edit` only warns and continues through lines
    1430-1433. Step 12 then clears the active pointer at lines 1442-1449 and
    declares completion. Thus an applicable downstream closeout failure is not
    recoverable through the new recordless path, despite the explicit contract
    that the pointer clear occurs only after downstream closeout succeeds. The
    helper test models `closeoutPr` as fail-closed, but the actual skill
    instructions do not implement that behavior.
  - Fix: For synced archive completion, make an applicable tracked-PR update a
    required closeout operation: a missing client or failed edit must stop before
    pointer clearing and leave the archive receipts available for recordless
    retry. If best-effort PR synchronization is intentionally desired instead,
    that is a product-contract change requiring explicit approval rather than a
    warning path. Add a test that injects PR closeout failure and proves the
    pointer and confirmation remain untouched, followed by a successful
    recordless retry.
  - Requirement: p02-t03 downstream closeout ordering and the fresh
    failure-recoverability contract.
