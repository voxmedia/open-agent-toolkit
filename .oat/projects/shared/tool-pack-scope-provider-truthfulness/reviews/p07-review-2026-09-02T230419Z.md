### Medium

- **An out-of-bounds observation envelope aborts the entire durable record
  write** (`packages/cli/src/providers/identity/runtime-observation.ts:38-56`,
  `packages/cli/src/commands/project/dispatch/record.ts:262`)
  - Issue: `MAX_ENVELOPE_ENTRIES` and the new `MAX_ENVELOPE_BYTES` are enforced
    by a throwing `.parse`, so exceeding either fails the whole command rather
    than degrading the optional layer. Measured against the real corpus, **58 of
    1,596** local Codex rollouts (3.6%) and 4 of 2,728 Claude transcripts exceed
    one of the two bounds; in my even sample, 13 of 250 rollouts did. Driven
    with a real 25 MB rollout:

    ```text
    bigrollout exit=1
    { "status": "error", "message": "[... too_big, maximum 5000, path entries ...]" }
    persisted? NONE
    ```

    Round 1 asked for the byte cap and it was correctly added, but neither
    round noticed that tripping an input bound on an explicitly optional,
    non-authoritative layer destroys a mandatory write. A caller that attaches
    a large transcript loses the dispatch record entirely.

  - Fix: separate the two failure classes. Keep content, provider and shape
    violations fail-closed, but treat an envelope _size_ violation as
    `{status: 'not-reported'}` plus a diagnostic, so the record still
    publishes. Alternatively project before bounding, since the projection
    reduces a 15 MB transcript to 475 KB and a 266 KB rollout to 2.3 KB in my
    measurements. Add a regression asserting the record persists with
    `not-reported` when the envelope is over-bound.
