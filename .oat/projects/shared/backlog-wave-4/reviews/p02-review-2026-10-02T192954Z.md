  - Issue: the stale decision is based on the read at line 718, but the later rename is unconditional. With a dead claim S, contender A reads S and pauses; B recovers S and returns `acquired`; A resumes and renames B's live claim away. Before A restores it, C can link its own claim and return `acquired`. A then ignores restoration's `EEXIST`, deletes B's saved claim, and returns `held` naming B while the shared path names C.
