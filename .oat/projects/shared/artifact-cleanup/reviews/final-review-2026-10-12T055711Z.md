### Medium

- **M1: Detect each fenced payload independently** (`packages/cli/src/commands/project/journal-check/rules.ts:779`). `fencedBlocks` groups consecutive `recordMask=false` lines, rather than individual fences. A `quoted-evidence` fence immediately followed by an unmarked dispatch payload exempts both; a four-space indented line immediately before a payload prevents the scanner from opening it. Both variants return `ok` at review and completion, while the isolated identical payload refuses with `dispatch-payload`. Parse each actual fence with its own opener, closer, info string and preceding marker; do not infer fence boundaries from runs of excluded lines. Keep individually marked evidence accepted and cover both compositions. This is a D6 refusal-contract defect, not a request to reject legitimate quoted incident evidence.

- **M2: Honor the entire digest's accepted line limit** (`packages/cli/src/commands/project/log/rollup.ts:386`). `maxLines` accepts any positive integer, but the final omission trailer and categorized-learning cross-reference are appended unconditionally after the fit check. Bound the final rendered blocks, or reject unsupported values explicitly through configuration; the existing minimum-useful-size documentation does not turn accepted values into a hard limit. Preserve full-set ledger graduation independently of what the digest can render. Requirement D4 and the documented whole-section bound are affected.
