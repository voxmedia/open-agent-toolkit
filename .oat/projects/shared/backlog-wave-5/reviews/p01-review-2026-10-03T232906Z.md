### Low

- **Kickoff stop list blurs an immediate stop** (`.agents/skills/oat-project-autonomous/SKILL.md:271`)
  - Issue: Step 1.5 lists "exact-target loss or accepted-launch failure after its owning identical-target continuation is exhausted". The owning contract (`oat-project-implement/references/phase-execution.md:244`) makes a lost or unbindable exact target a direction-required stop with no fallback, with nothing to exhaust first. The trailing clause can be read as applying to both items, and "identical-target continuation" is not a term the owning contract uses (it says "same-handle continuation" and "same exact target"). The shared contract and the docs page state the stop without this qualifier.
  - Suggestion: Split the clause: exact-target loss stops immediately; accepted-launch failure stops once same-handle continuation or the authorized fresh recover launch is unavailable. Use the owning contract's terms.
- **"Requires an existing blocking finding" reads as a pre-existing finding** (`.agents/agents/oat-reviewer.md:229`)
  - Issue: The sentence means "raise a finding using the existing blocking-finding model", as the ticket words it, but as written it says the finding must already exist. The same phrase is repeated in `.agents/skills/oat-project-review-provide/SKILL.md:633` and `.agents/skills/oat-project-review-provide-remote/SKILL.md:218`. The next sentence in the reviewer role recovers the meaning; the two skill briefs have no such sentence.
  - Suggestion: Reword all three to "requires a blocking finding under the existing severity model".
- **Reviewer instruction sits inside the artifact template body** (`.agents/agents/oat-reviewer.md:532`)
  - Issue: The new "Record changed-boundary probes…" paragraph was added inside the fenced review-artifact template under `## Verification Commands`. Every other instruction in that template is a `{placeholder}`; this one is bare prose, so a reviewer following the template literally copies the instruction into each artifact instead of the probe record.
  - Suggestion: Move the instruction to Step 8's prose, or express it as a placeholder such as `{changed-boundary probes: command, provenance, expected/observed outcome, limitation}`.
- **Two contract-test controls cannot fail** (`packages/cli/src/validation/skills.test.ts:3735`)
  - Issue: The `withoutStop` block deletes a sentence from a local string and then asserts the test's own regex no longer matches it; it exercises no shipped text and passes regardless of what the reviewer role says. At `packages/cli/src/validation/skills.test.ts:3790`, `not.toMatch(/may continue after a failed recovery/i)` only fails on that exact phrase, so any other wording of a continue-after-failure promise passes. Implementation notes cite the first as a "stop-clause deletion guard". The positive assertions around both are real and do pin the contract text.
  - Suggestion: Drop the `withoutStop` block, and either drop the negative match or replace it with a positive assertion that the kickoff section states continue-after-failure is a separate future policy.
- **Vendor traversal edges are unpinned, and deleting a vendored doc needs no bump** (`packages/cli/src/validation/skills.ts:1190`)
  - Issue: The top-level `tests/` exclusion and a chained link (one skill linking into another skill's vendored directory, as `oat-agent-instructions-apply` does today) have no fixture; a probe confirmed both work, but a regression in either would be silent. Separately, `--diff-filter=ACMR` at `packages/cli/src/validation/skills.ts:1230` means deleting a vendored shared doc requires no bump from its consumers (probe: 0 owners, consumer left with a dangling link). This matches how the gate already treats deletions inside a skill directory, so it meets the ticket's "same as a change under the skill's own directory" wording.
  - Suggestion: Add the chained-link and `tests/`-only cases to the existing real-Git fixture. Treat deletion as a recorded limitation or a follow-up backlog item, not a change for this phase.
