  - Issue: Structural bodies are checked only for newlines, while command-owned `##`/`###` marker rejection is applied only to judgment bodies at line 234. A structural body such as the exact pending-synthesis heading is therefore accepted. `entriesSection` then treats it as the end of the entries region, and `synthesizeProjectLog` uses an unscoped `content.indexOf(SYNTHESIS_PENDING_HEADING)` at `packages/cli/src/commands/project/log/synthesize.ts:112`, so it targets the spoofed body instead of the canonical section. A direct reproduction successfully appended that body and then showed synthesis deleting the structural body and the canonical tail. This violates the core valid-by-construction, append-only, byte-preserving, and roll-up-before-archive guarantees.
  - Fix: Enforce the same command-owned marker boundary for structural bodies, and make synthesis locate a unique canonical top-level synthesis section by structure rather than the first matching substring. Also protect synthesis bodies from recreating the pending marker. Add regression tests covering structural bodies equal to `## Entries` and the pending-synthesis heading, entry-byte preservation after synthesis, and a completed synthesis body containing marker-like text.
  - Requirement: Validated machine-parseable entry grammar; append-only entry preservation; safe synthesis.
